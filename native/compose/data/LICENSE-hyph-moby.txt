Additional format notes from the same document:

The hyphenation marks are indicated with ASCII 165
character, which is significant in alphabetization.

Note that words that ought *not* to be hyphenated are included for
completeness.

Folio's hyph-moby.bin is a generated, filtered, lowercase, front-coded version
of mhyph.txt, not the original distribution. The generator and exclusions are
documented in next/tools/gen-hyph-moby.py. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt for hashes.
